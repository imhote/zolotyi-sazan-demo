Pond philosophy

Catch & Release

“We don't count the kilos in the keepsack. We count the fish that made it back into the water.”

Learn more

Careful fish handling

Wet hands, minimal time out of the water, no sudden moves. Let the fish swim back on its own rather than throwing it in.

Carp mat

Weigh-ins and photos happen on the mat only. It protects the fish's scales and slime coat from damage.

Keep the spot clean

Leave your spot the way you found it. Take your rubbish with you or leave it in the bin provided.

Tents and quiet

Tents are welcome at open spots. Loud music and generators are not our style.
